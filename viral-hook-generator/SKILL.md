# Viral Hook Generator

Write hooks that make the right viewer stop scrolling, and that the video or post can actually deliver on.

## Ask first

Ask only what you can't infer, in one short message. Use the defaults if the user doesn't answer.

1. **Topic:** what is the video or post about? (required)
2. **Platform:** Shorts, Reels, TikTok, X or LinkedIn? Default: short-form video.
3. **Audience:** who should stop scrolling? Default: beginners curious about the topic.
4. **Payoff:** what will the viewer get by the end (a tip, a result, a story)? Default: one useful tip.
5. **Tone:** friendly, bold, funny or expert? Default: friendly and direct.

If the user already gave the topic and enough context, skip the questions and write.

## Workflow

1. Pin down the single payoff the content delivers. Every hook must point to it.
2. Write 10 hooks, at least one from each of these angles:
   - **Curiosity gap:** hint at the answer without giving it ("Nobody tells you this about…").
   - **Bold claim:** a strong, defensible statement.
   - **Mistake:** "Stop doing X" or "The mistake that…".
   - **Number:** a specific, true number or time frame.
   - **Question:** a question the viewer is already asking themselves.
   - **Story:** the first line of a short personal or customer story.
   - **Before/after:** a contrast between two states.
   - **Direct callout:** name the exact viewer ("If you freelance on Fiverr…").
3. Keep each hook speakable in about 3 seconds: under 15 words for video, under 25 for X and LinkedIn.
4. Pick the top 3 and say in one line why each works for this audience.

## Output format

```
## 10 hooks for: <topic> (<platform>)

1. <hook> (angle)
2. ...

## Top 3
1. <hook> — why it works
2. ...
3. ...

## On-screen text for the top pick
<4–6 words to show in the first frame>
```

## Quality bar

- Never promise something the content doesn't deliver. No clickbait the video can't pay off.
- No invented statistics, earnings, follower counts or results. If a number appears, it must come from the user.
- No false urgency ("only today", "before it's deleted") unless the user confirms it's true.
- Plain words a 12-year-old understands. No jargon unless the audience is expert.
- Match the platform: LinkedIn hooks can be a full sentence; TikTok hooks should be spoken and punchy.
